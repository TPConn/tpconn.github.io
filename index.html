2. Private Document Handling & Clinical Research Non-Affiliation Verified in Build 142

Because clinical research participants often manage sensitive personal records, Knight Innovations LLC provides the following explicit commitments regarding private documents in The Participant Connection (TPC), verified directly against the v1.2.40 (Build 142) application codebase:

  1. Personal Organization & Document-Management App: TPC is an independent personal organization, educational reference, and document-management application designed to help individual participants organize their own schedules, checklists, expenses, and personal documents.
  2. Knight Innovations LLC Does Not Conduct or Administer Clinical Research: Knight Innovations LLC does not conduct, sponsor, fund, recruit for, or administer clinical trials or medical research. Neither Knight Innovations LLC nor TPC is a clinical research facility, pharmaceutical sponsor, Contract Research Organization (CRO), hospital, Institutional Review Board (IRB) / Independent Ethics Committee (IEC), healthcare provider, or HIPAA-covered entity/business associate.
  3. No Institutional Informed Consent Form Administration: TPC does not create, issue, provide, collect, witness, or administer Informed Consent Forms (ICFs) for or on behalf of any clinical research facility, sponsor, or investigator.
  4. Voluntary Participant Uploads Only: Participants voluntarily choose whether to photograph, scan, upload, or import their own personal documents into TPC’s local on-device wallet and record tools. Document entry is entirely optional.
  5. Types of Personal Documents Participants May Store: Documents voluntarily added by the participant may include copies of Informed Consent Forms (ICFs), personal identification documents (such as a driver’s license or passport copy for travel reference), expense receipts, laboratory reports, medical history documents, or any other personal records chosen by the participant.
  6. No Collection or Access by Knight Innovations LLC: Verified against TPC v1.2.40 (Build 142), all private document images, imported PDFs, locally extracted OCR text, translated text, cropped/redacted copies, receipts, lab records, and notes are stored strictly inside the device’s private local application sandbox (AsyncStorage and sandboxed documentDirectory) and processed on-device. Private document contents are not collected, transmitted to, stored by, viewed, or accessed by Knight Innovations LLC.
  7. No Automatic Third-Party Sharing: Private documents stored in TPC are never automatically shared with clinical research facilities, sponsors, CROs, IRBs, healthcare providers, data brokers, advertisers, or unrelated third parties. On Android, automatic Android OS cloud backup is explicitly disabled (android:allowBackup="false", backup_rules.xml, and data_extraction_rules.xml).
  8. Full Participant Control Over Exports & Sharing: Participants retain sole control over whether and how any document leaves their device—for example, when explicitly saving or sharing a PDF, spreadsheet, cropped/redacted photo, encrypted backup archive, or user-selected text excerpt via the device’s Share Sheet, email app, or file picker.
  9. Optional Encrypted Backups Clearly Defined: Full backup creation is strictly user-initiated or user-opted-in, encrypted client-side with AES-256-GCM (TPC_ENC1) before leaving the app sandbox, and saved only to storage locations chosen by the participant (local device/external storage or, if explicitly opted in and active, the participant’s own private Google Drive Hidden App Data or private Apple iCloud Drive container—never Knight Innovations LLC servers). See Sections 4 and 5 for full technical details.
  10. Accurate Account, Billing, Diagnostic & Third-Party Disclosures: TPC does not create Knight Innovations LLC user accounts. Paid subscriptions are processed solely by Google Play Billing or Apple StoreKit 2 (Section 8). Bundled Android platform libraries (Google ML Kit / Google Play Services) may transmit standard technical SDK diagnostics to Google without transmitting your document photos or text, and user-initiated external services (driving route mileage lookup, Google Docs/Sheets resources, ChatGPT handoff, or optional public Oppie Demo Telegram preview links) operate strictly as disclosed in Sections 6 and 9.

4. Encrypted Backups, Legacy Restores & Unencrypted File Exports Active in v1.2.40 (Build 142)

A. Full Participant Data Backups (Mandatory Client-Side AES-256-GCM Encryption)

Whenever you create a full Data Backup in v1.2.40 (Build 142)—whether saved manually to device/external storage, shared via the OS Share Sheet, or uploaded via opt-in Cloud Backup—the archive is encrypted on your device before leaving local staging:

  • Cipher & Key Derivation: Client-side AES-256-GCM (TPC_ENC1 authenticated binary envelope) derived from your required Backup Password (minimum 10 characters, or an optional 24-character password from the built-in cryptographically secure generator) using 250,000 iterations of PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, a unique random 16-byte salt, a unique random 12-byte IV, header authentication (GCM AAD), and SHA-256 integrity checksums.
  • No Plaintext Full Backup Export: Unencrypted full backup creation is disabled in v1.2.40, and temporary staging files are deleted immediately after encryption. Manual saves via Android Storage Access Framework or iOS Files may carry a .zip extension for file-picker compatibility while containing the encrypted TPC_ENC1 payload; cloud backups use .tpcenc.
  • Passphrase Custody & Hardware Key Caching: For manual encrypted backups, your Backup Password is never saved by TPC and cannot be recovered by Knight Innovations LLC if forgotten. If—and only if—you explicitly enable Automatic Cloud Backup on your device, your backup passphrase is stored locally inside your device’s hardware-backed Android Keystore or Apple iOS Keychain so scheduled backups can run on that device without sending your passphrase to Knight Innovations LLC, Google, or Apple. To restore on a new device, you must enter your Backup Password yourself.

B. Legacy Unencrypted Backup Import Compatibility

So existing participants are never locked out of older archives created prior to encrypted backups, the Restore tool can still inspect and import previously created unencrypted legacy .zip archives when you explicitly choose one (with SHA-256 integrity and Zip-Slip path-traversal safety checks). All newly generated full backups in v1.2.40 are encrypted.

C. Participant-Initiated Individual File Exports & Shares (Unencrypted)

When you explicitly choose to generate and save, print, email, or share individual documents or summaries outside Full Data Backup—including:

  • Expense & Mileage Reports exported as PDF (.pdf), Excel (.xlsx), or CSV (.csv);
  • Health Measurements Exports generated offline as PDF (.pdf) or CSV (.csv);
  • ICF PDFs, Cropped/Redacted Document Images, or Extracted/Translated Text saved or shared via the system Share Sheet; or
  • Study Tracker Visit Summaries shared via email or native Share Sheet;

those individual files are exported as standard unencrypted files or text so you can view, print, submit, or share them with clinics, tax preparers, or family members. Once exported or shared outside the App, those files are under your custody and subject to the storage or recipient services you choose.

5. Optional Opt-In Encrypted Cloud Backup Off by Default — Opt-In Only

Automatic Cloud Backup is off by default. If you explicitly choose to authorize and enable it inside Data Backup & Restore, TPC uploads only your client-side AES-256-GCM encrypted .tpcenc archives (along with minimal non-content archive metadata such as timestamp, SHA-256 digest, and record counts) directly from your device to your own personal cloud storage:

  • Android — Google Drive Hidden App Data (drive.appdata): Uses Google Identity Authorization (play-services-auth) requesting strictly the least-privilege scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. TPC has zero visibility or access to any personal files, photos, or folders in your Google Drive—only its hidden app-private backup files (tpc_cloud_backup_latest.tpcenc and tpc_cloud_backup_prev.tpcenc). You may disconnect and revoke TPC’s OAuth token anytime inside the App or through your Google Account Connected Apps settings.
  • iOS & iPadOS — Private Apple iCloud Drive Container: Uses Apple’s private non-public CloudDocuments ubiquity container (iCloud.com.theparticipantconnection.app), stored exclusively in your signed-in Apple Account’s iCloud Drive.
  • Zero-Plaintext Upload Enforcement & Provisioning Note: Native guards verify the TPC_ENC1 cryptographic header and local SHA-256 checksum before any network transfer begins. Neither Knight Innovations LLC, Google, nor Apple receives unencrypted document contents or your Backup Password in cloud backup uploads. Activation of automatic Google Drive / iCloud Drive authorization requires active platform OAuth/iCloud entitlement configuration for the store release build; manual encrypted export to device storage or any storage provider you choose is always available.

7. Device Permissions & Biometric Privacy All Permissions Optional

Every runtime device permission in TPC is optional and requested in context when you choose the corresponding feature. Declining a permission does not block unrelated features of the App.

  • Camera & Photo Library / Document Files: Used strictly when you choose to photograph, scan, import, crop, redact, or export personal documents (such as Informed Consent pages, IDs, medical records, or expense receipts) or pick an encrypted backup archive to restore. Microphone permission is explicitly disabled (microphonePermission: false).
  • Notifications, Exact Visit Alarms & Clock Handoff: Used strictly to schedule, fire, and restore local on-device reminders for study visits, check-in/checkout and follow-up countdowns, and packing departure reminders (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, SET_ALARM, and iOS Time-Sensitive notifications when enabled). Overlay permissions (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW), USE_EXACT_ALARM, and third-party launcher-badge permissions are explicitly removed.
  • Calendar Access (Add to Calendar): Used only when you tap Add to Calendar. On iOS 17+, TPC uses write-only event sheets (NSCalendarsWriteOnlyAccessUsageDescription / EKEventEditViewController); on Android and earlier iOS versions, TPC opens the system calendar event composer without reading or harvesting existing calendar events.
  • Biometric Authentication & Screen Privacy Shield (Opt-In App Lock): When enabled by you, TPC delegates unlocking to your device’s native OS security framework (Android BiometricPrompt / KeyguardManager; iOS LocalAuthentication Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode). Knight Innovations LLC and TPC never access, read, store, or transmit biometric templates, fingerprints, facial scans, or device passcodes. When App Lock is active, TPC enables Android FLAG_SECURE (blocking screenshots and recent-apps previews) and an iOS multitasking privacy shield overlay.

10. Data Retention, Deletion, Children’s Privacy & Your Rights

Data Retention & How to Delete Everything

  • Local Device Records & Documents: Retained strictly on your device under your sole control until you delete individual records inside the App, clear App Storage in your device Settings (e.g., Android Settings → Apps → The Participant Connection → Storage → Clear storage), or uninstall TPC. Because Knight Innovations LLC holds no server copy of your local records or documents, clearing storage or uninstalling permanently erases local data unless you previously saved an encrypted backup.
  • On-Device Recently Deleted Bin: Deleted Expense Tracker items move to a local Recently Deleted recovery bin (30-day recovery window) until purged or until App storage is cleared.
  • Manual & Cloud Backups: Manually exported backup files remain wherever you saved them until you delete them via your file manager. Opt-in encrypted .tpcenc backups in your Google Drive Hidden App Data or Apple iCloud Drive remain in your personal cloud account until you disconnect or remove them via Backup & Restore controls, Google Account Connected Apps, or Apple iCloud Storage Settings.
  • Direct Support Emails: If you email theparticipantconnection@gmail.com for support or privacy assistance, Knight Innovations LLC uses your email address and message solely to respond to your request and deletes correspondence upon request unless retention is legally required.

Children’s Privacy

The Participant Connection is intended exclusively for adult clinical research participants (ages 18 and older) and is not directed to children under age 13 (or under age 16 where local law applies). Knight Innovations LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, please contact theparticipantconnection@gmail.com for prompt deletion.

U.S. & International Privacy Rights (New Jersey, U.S. State Privacy Laws, EEA/UK GDPR, Switzerland, Japan & Brazil LGPD)

Because Knight Innovations LLC does not operate central participant accounts or collect your private documents onto developer servers, you exercise direct access, correction, portability, and deletion rights directly inside the App (editing records, encrypted backup/restore, PDF/CSV/Excel exports, cloud disconnect, and local storage erasure). Where applicable data-protection laws (including U.S. state privacy statutes such as the New Jersey Data Privacy Act, EEA/UK GDPR, Swiss FADP, Japan APPI, or Brazil LGPD) apply to support communications or inquiries, you may contact Knight Innovations LLC anytime to request access to or deletion of email correspondence or to raise any privacy question, and you retain the right to lodge a complaint with your applicable data-protection supervisory authority.
