At-a-Glance Privacy Summary

  • No Accounts & No Developer Servers: You do not need to create an account, register an email address, or sign in to use the App, and we operate no backend servers or cloud databases that receive, store, or view your clinical study records, appointments, checklists, health logs, or Informed Consent Form (ICF) photographs.
  • Local On-Device Storage: All participant records and photographed consent pages remain stored locally inside the App's private Android storage on your device (with automatic Android cloud backup disabled).

1. Overview & Scope

The Participant Connection ("TPC," "the App," "we," "us," or "our") is an independent educational and personal organization mobile application created to help clinical research participants stay informed, organized, and prepared throughout their research experience (Inform • Support • Empower).

This Privacy Policy applies to The Participant Connection Android application distributed under package identifier com.theparticipantconnection.app. It explains what information you may store locally inside the App, how Android device permissions are used, what limited technical diagnostics are processed by bundled platform libraries (specifically Google ML Kit), how user-initiated backups and external links work, and how you control or delete your information at any time.

2. Independent Educational Tool & Non-Affiliation Notice

The Participant Connection is an independent participant-support application. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or operated on behalf of any pharmaceutical sponsor, Contract Research Organization (CRO), Clinical Research Unit (CRU), hospital, Institutional Review Board (IRB) / Independent Ethics Committee (IEC), or government agency. The App does not conduct clinical trials, recruit or enroll human subjects into research protocols, transmit electronic patient-reported outcomes (ePRO) to researchers, or provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

When you use The Participant Connection, any personal or study-related information you enter or capture is stored locally within the App's private Android application sandbox (AsyncStorage and /data/user/0/com.theparticipantconnection.app/files/) on your own device:

  • Study Schedule & Contact Records (My Study Tracker): Study or protocol titles, investigational product labels, clinic/site names, addresses, phone numbers, email contacts, screening dates, in-house stay dates, follow-up appointments, washout eligibility dates, registry call reminders, personal dosing logs, compensation/stipend notes, and personal visit notes you choose to enter.
  • Informed Consent Form Scans & Text (ICF Scanner & Wallet): Photographs or imported images of Informed Consent Form (ICF) pages, locally generated page thumbnails, on-device Optical Character Recognition (OCR) text extracted from those images, on-device translated text, and personal annotations.
  • Personal Health & Vitals Logs (Health Measurements & BMI): Self-entered blood pressure measurements (systolic, diastolic, pulse), weight, height, calculated Body Mass Index (BMI), measurement dates/notes, and unit preferences (Imperial or Metric).
  • Packing Checklists & Travel Preparation (What to Bring): Master Study Checklist selections, custom checklist items, personal packing lists, section visibility preferences, travel departure dates/modes/notes, and local departure-day reminder times.
  • App Preferences & Local Recovery Snapshot: Selected interface language (English, Español, Deutsch, 中文, 日本語, or Français), onboarding/disclaimer acknowledgments, active tab preferences, and an internal local recovery snapshot maintained strictly inside the App's private device directory to protect against accidental state loss during app updates.

Automatic Cloud Backup Disabled: To prevent silent or unintended cloud uploads of sensitive consent documents or study notes, the Android application is configured with android:allowBackup="false" and strict Android data-extraction exclusion rules (backup_rules.xml and data_extraction_rules.xml), excluding your App records from automatic Android cloud backups and unauthenticated device-to-device transfers.

4. Android Permissions & How They Are Used

All runtime permissions are optional; if you decline a permission, unrelated features of the App continue to function normally.

Android Permission Purpose in The Participant Connection
Camera (android.permission.CAMERA) Requested only when you choose to photograph Informed Consent Form (ICF) pages directly into your local ICF Wallet.
Photo Selection / Legacy Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE up to Android 12, API 32) Allows importing existing document images or saving user-exported files on older Android versions. On Android 13+, image selection uses the Android system picker without broad media-library access.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM), Boot Recovery (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED), & Vibration (VIBRATE) Used solely to schedule, display, and restore local on-device reminders you set for study visits, washout completion dates, registry calls, and packing departure reminders. Reminders are generated locally on your phone, not sent from a remote push server.
Set Alarm (com.android.alarm.permission.SET_ALARM) Used only when you tap to set an alarm in your phone's Clock app; opens your system Clock app pre-filled with the time and label so you can review and confirm saving the alarm yourself.
Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) Used by Google ML Kit to download offline OCR and translation language models from Google servers when needed (see Section 5), and to verify connectivity before model downloads.

5. On-Device OCR, Translation & Third-Party SDK Disclosures (Google ML Kit)

The App integrates Google ML Kit (com.google.android.gms:play-services-mlkit-text-recognition and com.google.mlkit:translate) to provide offline-capable Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and six-language translation inside the ICF Scanner & Wallet:

  • Your Document Photos & Text Stay On-Device: Image recognition and text translation execute locally on your Android device. Neither The Participant Connection nor Google ML Kit uploads your ICF photographs, extracted OCR text, or translated document text to external servers.
  • Language & Vision Model Downloads: Google Play Services and Google ML Kit connect over encrypted HTTPS to Google servers to download or update on-device OCR recognition modules and neural translation language packs (for example, the first time you translate into Spanish, German, Chinese, Japanese, or French).
  • Google ML Kit SDK Operational Diagnostics: As documented in Google's official ML Kit Android Data Disclosure, Google's bundled ML Kit / Google Play Services libraries (com.google.android.datatransport) automatically collect limited technical diagnostics and device metadata — such as device model, Android OS version, application package/version, SDK configuration, model download events, API performance/error metrics, and technical device/installation identifiers — encrypted in transit over HTTPS to Google for SDK reliability, security, and performance analytics. We do not receive or access this Google SDK telemetry and cannot link it to any individual user. Google's processing of SDK diagnostics is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Technical Note on Notification Libraries: The App uses the standard expo-notifications library strictly for local on-device notifications. Although the underlying library merges standard Firebase Cloud Messaging receiver declarations into the compiled Android manifest, the App connects to no Firebase project, bundles no google-services.json credentials, and never requests remote push notification tokens.

6. Participant-Initiated Data Backup & Restore (.zip Archives)

The App includes a Data Backup & Restore tool allowing you to export all local App records and ICF Wallet images into a portable .zip archive (TPC_Backup_YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM.zip) or restore a previously exported archive:

  • Initiated Exclusively by You: Backup files are created, saved (via the Android system file picker), or shared (via the Android Share Sheet) only when you explicitly initiate an export or share action.
  • Integrity Checksums vs. Encryption: Each backup archive includes SHA-256 cryptographic checksums (manifest.json) to detect file corruption before restoration. However, exported .zip archives are standard, unencrypted ZIP files so you can inspect or move them across devices without proprietary lock-in.
  • Safeguarding Exported Files: Once you save or share a backup .zip file outside the App's private sandbox (such as to your Downloads folder, a computer, USB drive, email, or third-party cloud storage), anyone with access to that file or storage account could read its contents. Protecting exported backup archives is your responsibility and subject to the privacy practices of any storage or sharing service you choose.

7. Optional User-Initiated External Apps, Links & Handoffs

Certain optional convenience buttons allow you to open external applications or websites on your device. No information leaves the App unless you actively initiate one of these features:

  • External AI Excerpt Review (https://chatgpt.com/): In the ICF Scanner & Wallet, you may optionally redact common identifiers, copy selected text to your clipboard, and open ChatGPT in your browser or app. Before doing so, the App prompts you to review and remove personal identifiers (such as your full name, signature, date of birth, or participant ID). Any text you paste into an external AI service is governed by that provider's own privacy policy and terms.
  • Educational Guides & Community Spreadsheets (Google Docs / Google Sheets): Selecting external reference guides or clinic/travel spreadsheets in the Resources tab opens public Google Docs or Google Sheets links in your browser or Google app, subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Calendar, Maps, Clock, Phone & Email Handoffs: Tapping "Add to Calendar" (device Calendar app or calendar.google.com), clinic map search (device Maps app or google.com/maps), "Set Alarm" (system Clock app), email sharing (expo-mail-composer / mailto:), or phone call buttons (tel:, including clinic numbers you save or the optional Verified Clinical Trials phone shortcut) hands off only the specific details required for that action directly to the app you choose.

9. Data Retention, Access & Deletion Rights

Because all personal study data, health logs, checklists, and ICF images reside locally on your device, you have direct, immediate control over your data at all times:

  • View, Edit & Export: You may view, edit, or export your saved records inside the App at any time.
  • Selective Deletion: You can delete individual study appointments, health measurements, packing lists, or scanned ICF documents directly within the App.
  • Complete Local Erasure: You can permanently erase all local App data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → The Participant Connection → Storage → Clear data / Clear storage, or by uninstalling the App.
  • Exported Backup Files: Clearing App storage or uninstalling the App does not automatically delete TPC_Backup_*.zip files that you previously saved to your device's Downloads folder or external storage; delete those .zip files manually using your file manager whenever desired.

Because we do not maintain user accounts or store your personal data on developer servers, we hold no server-side copy of your study records to access, modify, or delete on your behalf.
