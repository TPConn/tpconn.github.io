2. Information You Enter & Store Locally on Your Device Active in v1.2.40

Everything you enter in TPC is stored locally inside your device’s private application sandbox (AsyncStorage and the app’s sandboxed documentDirectory). TPC developers cannot view, access, or retrieve these records.

  • My Study Tracker: Clinical study titles, protocol labels, clinic names, clinic addresses, clinic phone numbers and email addresses, screening dates, check-in/checkout dates, follow-up visit dates, personal reminders, notification timing preferences (including visit alarms and countdown reminders), and notes.
  • ICF Scanner & Wallet: Photographed or scanned Informed Consent Form (ICF) page images, imported PDFs or photos, locally extracted Optical Character Recognition (OCR) text, translated text, cropped or permanently pixel-redacted copies you save, and personal notes.
  • My Lab Reports, Medical History & Study Medications: User-entered screening and study laboratory values, imported medical documents/PDFs/images, diagnosis and surgical history notes, study medication names, dosages, logs, and drug half-life reference records.
  • Expense & Mileage Tracker: Clinical study trips, separate personal trips, trip summaries, expense line items (lodging, flights, rental cars, mileage, fuel/tolls/parking, rideshare/taxi, meals, and custom categories), camera-captured or imported receipt images/PDFs, tax-recordkeeping flags, and recoverable items in Recently Deleted.
  • Tools, Measurements & Checklists: Blood pressure (including measurement arm preference), heart rate, temperature, weight, BMI calculations, blood glucose readings and unit preferences, What to Bring checklists, Personal Packing Lists, Restricted Items references, and Shopping Lists.
  • App Preferences & Local Entitlement State: Selected interface language, User Agreement acceptance timestamp, optional App Lock setting, optional automatic cloud backup preferences, and store subscription entitlement cache (including a local Keystore/Keychain entitlement verification record on-device).
OS Device Auto-Backup & OTA Code Updates Disabled On Android, automatic Android OS Auto Backup is explicitly disabled (android:allowBackup="false" along with backup_rules.xml and data_extraction_rules.xml) so plaintext clinical study records are not automatically uploaded into generic device backups. Remote over-the-air JavaScript code updates (Expo Updates) are also disabled (enabled: false).

3. Encrypted Backups, Legacy Restores & Unencrypted File Exports Active in v1.2.40

A. Full Participant Data Backups (Mandatory Client-Side AES-256-GCM Encryption)

Whenever you create a full Data Backup in v1.2.40—whether saved manually to device storage, shared via the OS Share Sheet, or uploaded via opt-in Cloud Backup—the archive is encrypted on your device before leaving staging:

  • Cipher & Key Derivation: AES-256-GCM (TPC_ENC1 authenticated binary envelope) using a key derived from your required password/passphrase (minimum 10 characters, or an optional 24-character password created with the built-in cryptographically secure random password generator) with 250,000 iterations of PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, a unique random 16-byte salt, a unique random 12-byte IV, and header authentication (GCM AAD), plus SHA-256 integrity checksums.
  • Zero Plaintext Export for Full Backups: Unencrypted full backup creation is disabled in v1.2.40, and temporary staging files are deleted immediately after encryption. (Manual local saves via Android Storage Access Framework or iOS Files may still carry a .zip filename for file-picker compatibility while containing the encrypted TPC_ENC1 payload; cloud backups use .tpcenc.)
  • Passphrase Responsibility & Hardware Key Caching: For manual encrypted backups, your password is never saved by TPC and cannot be recovered if lost. If—and only if—you choose to enable Automatic Cloud Backup on your device, your authorized backup passphrase is stored locally inside your device’s hardware-backed Android Keystore or Apple iOS Keychain so scheduled backups can run silently on that device without sending your passphrase to TPC, Google, or Apple. To restore onto a replacement device, you must still enter your Backup Password yourself.

B. Legacy Unencrypted Backup Import Compatibility

So longtime participants are never locked out of older archives, the Restore tool can still inspect and import previously created unencrypted legacy .zip backup archives from earlier versions of the App when you explicitly select one (with SHA-256 integrity and path-traversal safety checks). All newly exported full backups in v1.2.40 are encrypted.

C. Non-Archive File Exports & Shares (Reports, PDFs, Spreadsheets & Redacted Copies)

When you explicitly choose to generate and save, print, email, or share individual summaries or documents outside Full Data Backup—such as:

  • Expense & Mileage Reports exported as PDF (.pdf), Excel (.xlsx), or CSV (.csv);
  • Health Measurements Exports generated offline as PDF (.pdf) or CSV (.csv);
  • ICF PDFs, Cropped/Redacted Photos, or Extracted/Translated Text saved or shared via the system Share Sheet; or
  • Study Tracker Visit Summaries shared by email or native share menu;

those individual files are exported as standard unencrypted files or text so you can view, print, submit, or share them with clinics, tax preparers, or family members. Once exported or shared outside the App, those files are governed by where you store them and whom you share them with.

4. Optional Opt-In Encrypted Cloud Backup Opt-In Only

Automatic Cloud Backup is off by default. If you explicitly turn it on inside Data Backup & Restore, TPC uploads only your client-side AES-256-GCM encrypted .tpcenc archives (plus small encrypted-archive metadata such as timestamp, SHA-256 hash, and record counts) directly from your device to your own personal cloud storage:

  • Android — Google Drive Hidden App Data (drive.appdata): Uses Google Identity Authorization requesting strictly the least-privilege scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. TPC has zero visibility or access to any of your personal documents, photos, or folders in Google Drive—only its hidden app-private backup files (tpc_cloud_backup_latest.tpcenc and tpc_cloud_backup_prev.tpcenc). You can disconnect and revoke TPC’s OAuth token anytime directly in the App or in your Google Account permissions.
  • iOS & iPadOS — Private Apple iCloud Drive Container: Uses Apple’s private CloudDocuments ubiquity container (iCloud.com.theparticipantconnection.app, marked non-public), accessible only inside your signed-in Apple Account’s iCloud Drive under iOS Settings.
  • Zero-Plaintext Upload Enforcement: Native guards verify the TPC_ENC1 cryptographic header and local SHA-256 digest before any network transfer begins. Neither TPC, Google, nor Apple receives your unencrypted study records or your backup password inside cloud backup uploads.

6. Device Permissions & Biometric Privacy All Permissions Optional

Every device permission in TPC is optional and requested in context when you choose the related feature. If you decline any permission, all unrelated features of TPC continue operating normally.

  • Camera & Photo Library / Document Files: Used strictly when you capture, import, crop, redact, or export Informed Consent pages, medical records, or expense receipts, or select a backup archive to restore. Microphone permission is explicitly disabled.
  • Notifications & Exact Visit Alarms: Used strictly to fire local on-device reminders for study visits, check-in/checkout and follow-up countdowns, and packing lists (including restoring local alarms after device reboot on Android, and Time-Sensitive notifications on iOS when permitted). Overlay permissions (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) and launcher badge permissions are disabled.
  • Calendar Access (Add to Calendar): Used only when you tap Add to Calendar on a study visit or travel event. On iOS 17+, TPC uses write-only calendar event sheets (NSCalendarsWriteOnlyAccessUsageDescription / EKEventEditViewController); on Android and earlier iOS versions, TPC launches the native calendar event sheet without reading or harvesting your existing calendar events.
  • Biometric Authentication & Screen Privacy Shield (Opt-In App Lock): When enabled, TPC uses your device operating system’s native security framework (Android BiometricPrompt / KeyguardManager; iOS LocalAuthentication Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode). TPC never accesses, reads, stores, or transmits biometric templates, fingerprints, facial scans, or device passcodes. When App Lock is active, TPC also turns on Android FLAG_SECURE (blocking screenshots and recents thumbnails) and an iOS multitasking privacy shield overlay.

9. Data Retention, Deletion, Children’s Privacy & Your Rights

How Long Data Is Retained & How to Delete Everything

  • Local Device Data: Retained on your device solely under your control until you delete individual records in the App, clear App Storage in your device Settings, or uninstall TPC. Because TPC keeps no copy of your study data on developer servers, uninstalling or clearing storage permanently erases your local records unless you first create and save an encrypted backup.
  • Deleted Items Recovery Bin: In Expense Tracker, deleted items move to an on-device Recently Deleted bin until you permanently purge them or delete the app.
  • Cloud Backups: Encrypted .tpcenc backups in your Google Drive Hidden App Data or Apple iCloud Drive remain in your personal cloud account until you disconnect/remove them via your Google Account Connected Apps, Apple iCloud Storage Settings, or Backup & Restore controls.

Health & Regulatory Classification (Not a HIPAA Covered Entity)

TPC is an independent personal-organization and educational utility built for research participants directly. TPC is not a clinical research site, sponsor, Institutional Review Board (IRB), healthcare provider, or HIPAA-covered entity/business associate. You remain the sole custodian of the records you store on your device.

Children’s Privacy

The Participant Connection is designed for adult clinical research participants (ages 18+) and is not directed to children under age 13 (or under age 16 where local law applies). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Regional Privacy Rights (United States, EEA/UK GDPR, Brazil LGPD & Others)

Because TPC does not maintain developer-hosted user accounts or central participant databases, you exercise direct access, portability, correction, and deletion rights directly inside the App (through record editing, encrypted backup/restore, PDF/CSV/Excel export, cloud disconnect, and app storage deletion). You may also contact us anytime regarding privacy questions or assistance exercising your rights.

Policy Updates

If we add new online capabilities, analytics, or live services such as TPC Study Opps 👀 in a future release, we will update this page with a new Effective & Last Updated date and highlight material changes inside the App before they take effect.
